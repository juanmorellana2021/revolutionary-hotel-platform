## 🚨 Important Considerations

### **Regulatory (Payments):**

If you integrate Ruby payments, you need to understand:

**Peru Financial Regulations:**
- Money transmitter license (may be required)
- Tax reporting (IGV - Peru's VAT)
- AML/KYC compliance
- Consumer protection laws

**Good News:**
- Ruby handles most compliance
- They're licensed/regulated
- You're just integrating their service
- Similar to using Stripe or PayPal

**Action:** Consult with Peruvian lawyer specializing in fintech before launching payments.

---

### **Data Ownership:**

**Make sure contract specifies:**
- Who owns hotel data (you or HotelRunner)
- Who owns guest data
- Can hotels export their data?
- What happens if you cancel service?
- Can HotelRunner contact your hotels directly?

**Ideal terms:**
- Data owned by hotels
- You control hotel relationships
- HotelRunner is infrastructure provider only
- White-label so hotels see AiniTravel brand

---

### **Technical Dependencies:**

**Risks:**
- If HotelRunner API goes down, your hotels can't sync
- If HotelRunner shuts down (unlikely), you lose channel connections
- You're dependent on their roadmap

**Mitigation:**
- Build fallback: Manual booking entry
- SLA guarantee in contract (99.9% uptime)
- Diversify: Consider adding direct Booking.com integration too
- Insurance: Keep 2-3 critical channels direct
