## 📈 VALUE DRIVERS

### What Increases Valuation?

**Operational:**
1. **User Growth:** More users = higher multiples (10k → 100k → 1M)
2. **GMV (Gross Merchandise Value):** Booking volume shows scale
3. **Margin Expansion:** Higher profit margins = premium valuation
4. **Geographic Expansion:** Multi-country = "platform" not "local business"
5. **B2B Revenue:** Enterprise contracts = predictable recurring revenue
6. **Retention:** Low churn = sustainable growth

**Crypto-Specific:**
1. **Token Price:** ANTC appreciation directly increases founder wealth
2. **DEX Listing:** Public trading = price discovery + liquidity
3. **TVL (Total Value Locked):** DeFi features increase ecosystem value
4. **Holder Count:** 10k holders = community, 100k = movement
5. **Trading Volume:** High volume = real utility, not speculation
6. **Partnerships:** Integrations with other protocols = network effects

**Strategic:**
1. **Network Effects:** Each new partner/user increases value exponentially
2. **Brand Recognition:** Top-of-mind for "crypto + travel"
3. **Regulatory Compliance:** Clean legal structure enables acquisition
4. **Technology IP:** Proprietary tech = defensible moat
5. **Team Quality:** Experienced team attracts better offers

## 🚨 RISK FACTORS

### What Decreases Valuation?

**Market Risks:**
- Crypto winter (80% price decline kills token value)
- Travel recession (COVID-like event)
- Competition from Booking/Expedia launching crypto
- Regulatory crackdown on travel + crypto

**Operational Risks:**
- Failed marketing (no user acquisition)
- High churn (users leave after first booking)
- Partner defection (hotels stop accepting coins)
- Technical failures (hacks, downtime)

**Financial Risks:**
- Unsustainable burn rate (run out of cash)
- Margin compression (competition drives fees down)
- Reserve fund insolvency (bank run scenario)
- Currency fluctuations (USD/PEN risk)

**Mitigation:**
- Diversified revenue streams (not just crypto)
- Conservative financial planning
- Strong legal/compliance foundation
- Focus on fundamentals (users, revenue, profit)
