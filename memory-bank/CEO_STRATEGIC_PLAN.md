## 💰 THE HONEST ECONOMICS

### Where the $15 Spread Goes:

**Example: $100 Hotel Booking**
```
User pays: $100 in AiNi Rewards

Hotel charges us: $85 (15% discount for bulk/partnership)

Breakdown of $15 spread:
├─ Platform fee (us): $3 (3%)
├─ Payment processing: $2.50 (2.5% - Niubiz)
├─ Marketing/growth: $4 (4%)
├─ User cashback: $3 (3% rewards)
├─ Reserve fund: $2 (2%)
└─ Operating costs: $0.50 (0.5%)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TOTAL: $15

Our NET margin: $3 per $100 booking (3%)
```
