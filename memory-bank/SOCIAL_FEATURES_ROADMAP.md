### What Travelers Actually Struggle With:

**1. ❌ "I don't know what hikes/places exist"**
```
Problem: Guidebooks suck, Google is overwhelming
Current solution: Ask hotel staff (hit or miss)
Traveler ends up: Missing 80% of cool places
```

**2. ❌ "I'm lonely / Want to meet other travelers"**
```
Problem: Solo travel is isolating
Current solution: Awkwardly approach strangers at hostel
Traveler ends up: Eating dinner alone every night
```

**3. ❌ "Hotel is OK but not perfect"**
```
Problem: Want to change hotels but don't know where
Current solution: Book randomly on Booking, risk another bad choice
Traveler ends up: Staying in mediocre hotel (afraid to switch)
```

**4. ❌ "Bored at night / Don't know what's happening"**
```
Problem: No idea what events/parties/meetups are tonight
Current solution: Walk around aimlessly, end up at tourist trap
Traveler ends up: In bed by 9pm scrolling Instagram
```

**Booking.com solves:** NONE of these
**AiNi solves:** ALL of them

```javascript

  (Date Overlap × 20%) +

  (Age Similarity × 10%)
```
