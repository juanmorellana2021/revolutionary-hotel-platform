## Letter to HotelRunner

Dear HotelRunner Partnership Team,

I am writing to explore a technical partnership opportunity between our companies. We are developing an innovative Property Management System (PMS) that we plan to offer to hotels across Peru and Latin America as part of our comprehensive hospitality technology package.

### About Our Platform

**AiNi Travel** is building a revolutionary hotel management ecosystem that combines:
- **Property Management System (PMS)** - Core hotel operations
- **Channel Manager** - Multi-platform booking distribution
- **Revenue Management** - AI-powered pricing optimization
- **Guest Experience Platform** - Social travel network
- **Financial Management** - Accounting, payroll, and reporting
- **WhatsApp Integration** - Automated booking and guest communication

Our platform is currently operational at **Samay Wasi Pisac** in Cusco, Peru, and we are preparing to scale to multiple properties across the region.

### Why We Need HotelRunner API Access

We are seeking to integrate HotelRunner's Channel Manager API into our PMS solution to provide our hotel clients with:

1. **Seamless OTA Distribution** - Connect to Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, and 100+ channels
2. **Real-Time Inventory Sync** - Automatic room availability updates across all platforms
3. **Centralized Booking Management** - All reservations in one unified system
4. **Rate Parity Control** - Consistent pricing across distribution channels
5. **Reduced Manual Work** - Eliminate double-entry and booking errors

### Our Business Model

We will offer HotelRunner as a **core component** of our PMS package to hotel clients:

- **Target Market:** Small to medium-sized hotels (5-50 rooms) in Peru, Ecuador, Colombia, and Bolivia
- **Initial Launch:** 10 hotels in Cusco region (2026 Q1)
- **12-Month Goal:** 50+ hotel properties
- **Pricing Model:** Bundled SaaS subscription ($150-500/month per property)
- **Value Proposition:** Complete hotel tech stack with single login, unified data, local support

Each hotel client we onboard will become a HotelRunner customer through our platform integration.

### Technical Integration Requirements

We are requesting access to the following HotelRunner API capabilities:

**Essential APIs:**
- Room inventory management (create, update, availability)
- Rate plans and pricing updates
- Reservation retrieval and management
- Channel mapping and distribution controls
- Real-time booking notifications (webhooks)

**Preferred Integration Method:**
- RESTful API with OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Webhook support for real-time updates
- Sandbox environment for development and testing
- API documentation and technical support
- White-label or co-branded solution options

### Our Technical Capabilities

Our development team has extensive experience with:
- API integrations (Twilio, Booking.com, payment gateways)
- Real-time systems (WebSocket, Socket.IO)
- Multi-tenant SaaS architecture
- PostgreSQL and MySQL databases
- Node.js, PHP, Python backend development
- Mobile-responsive web applications

We have already built integrations with Twilio for WhatsApp automation and are currently developing connections to Booking.com's official API.

1. **Expanded Market Reach** - Access to Peru and Latin American hotels through our sales channel
2. **Recurring Revenue** - Each new PMS client becomes a long-term HotelRunner subscription
3. **Market Development** - We handle local sales, onboarding, training, and support in Spanish
4. **Product Feedback** - Direct insights from hospitality operators in emerging markets
