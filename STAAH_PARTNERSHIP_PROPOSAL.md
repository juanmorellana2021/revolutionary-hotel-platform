- **Target Market:** 50+ independent hotels in Peru, Ecuador, Colombia, Bolivia (Year 1)
- **Market Gap:** Small hotels (5-50 rooms) lack access to professional channel management
- **Revenue Model:** Integrated PMS + Channel Manager subscription package
- **Competitive Advantage:** Local expertise, Spanish support, affordable pricing

## 🤝 Proposed Partnership Model

### Partnership Structure: Authorized Reseller

**How It Works:**
1. AiNi Travel becomes authorized STAAH reseller for Latin American market
2. We integrate STAAH Channel Manager into our PMS offering
3. We sell bundled "AiNi Travel Complete" package to hotel clients
4. Hotels pay us one monthly fee for PMS + Channel Manager
5. We pay STAAH monthly reseller fee per active property
6. Both companies benefit from long-term recurring revenue

### Revenue Sharing Options

**Option A - Per-Property Reseller Fee:**
- AiNi Travel pays STAAH fixed monthly fee per connected property
- Allows us to control pricing and packaging flexibility
- Predictable costs enable competitive market pricing
- Example: STAAH charges us $X/property, we charge hotels $Y total

**Option B - Revenue Share:**
- Percentage of monthly subscription revenue goes to STAAH
- Scales naturally with our pricing tiers (basic, standard, premium)
- Aligned incentives - both companies win when hotels succeed
- Example: 30-40% of monthly subscription allocated to channel management

**Option C - Tiered Volume Pricing:**
- Per-property cost decreases as we add more hotels
- Rewards our growth with better margins
- Encourages aggressive expansion
- Example: 
  - 1-10 properties: $X per property
  - 11-25 properties: $Y per property (lower)
  - 26-50 properties: $Z per property (even lower)

### Value Proposition for Hotels

**What Hotels Pay (Monthly Subscription):**
- Basic Tier: $75/month (5-10 room properties)
- Standard Tier: $125/month (11-25 room properties)
- Premium Tier: $175/month (26-50 room properties)

**What Hotels Get:**
- Complete PMS (front desk, housekeeping, accounting, payroll)
- STAAH Channel Manager (2000+ OTA connections)
- WhatsApp booking automation
- Payment processing integration
- Ongoing support in Spanish
- Training and onboarding

**Competitive Comparison:**
- Traditional PMS alone: $100-300/month
- Channel Manager alone: $50-150/month
- **Our Bundle:** $75-175/month (40-50% savings!)
