How to Add Real Room Photos:

  1. Go to Room Management in your manager dashboard
  2. Edit any room
  3. Upload photos using the photo upload section
  4. Mark one photo as "Primary" - this will show in the calendar
  5. The calendar will automatically display the primary photo
